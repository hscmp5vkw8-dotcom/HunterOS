## Acceptance criteria

- Product import returns only available public metadata: name, brand, explicit model/SKU/category, price and currency, image, weight and unit, source URL and attribution. Unknown values remain blank. Non-USD prices are retained as source references and never treated as USD.
- JSON-LD Product/ProductGroup/graphs, selected variants and product metadata fallback are supported. Variant ambiguity is visible; variant-specific price, SKU, image and weight are withheld until selected. Pages without a photo can still import other details.
- Review occurs before applying to editable private gear. Applying intentionally fills the form; subsequent edits remain private. Sharing requires a separate explicit action and publishes only server-derived metadata. Source references survive workspace backup/restore. Canonical/variant duplicates are detected; repeated taps cannot duplicate saves or requests.
- Public HTTP(S) product pages from manufacturers or retailers are supported. Every redirect receives fresh lexical and public-DNS checks; native TCP connects to the validated IP while TLS verifies the original hostname. Credentials/access tokens, custom ports, malformed/private/reserved hosts, mixed DNS answers, non-HTML/large responses, timeouts and blocked pages fail safely. No caller cookies or authorization are sent to vendors. Page scripts and instructions never execute; no bypass or paid scraper is used.
- Source facts distinguish brand, explicit manufacturer, seller and explicit parent company; hostname alone never establishes a maker. Identity evidence stays page-stated or unknown and needs legal review. Net/product/pack weights remain distinct from shipping weight. Pack size, quoted serving/nutrition, ingredients and allergens import only when explicitly present, can be edited privately, and survive backup. Serving calories/grams are never silently multiplied into package totals.
- Saving a reviewed import collects maker/brand source identities in a deduplicated private outreach list. Retailers are not assumed to be manufacturers. Unknown identities remain needs-review with source links. The queue is preserved in private workspace backups and never included in shared product publication or friend messages. No contact, enrollment, verified affiliation or commission is implied.
- Central inbox is prominent from Home. Search/unread filters work without new account discovery. Existing bilateral block, participant-only history, removal/read-only behavior, account-change/signout clearing and send idempotency remain enforced. No actual friendships or blocks are changed.
- Food ideas and two simple snack recipes can be added as private consumables to locker or selected trip/loadout with quantity, calories, price and weight left unknown for the user to enter. Practical allergy/storage guidance avoids personalized medical advice.
- Off-road shows ten suggested essentials, clearly described as suggestions without community usage rankings. First-aid catalog discovery works even without off-road tags. Product website links retain existing source attribution and explicitly have no verified referral/affiliate benefit.
- Type checks, full client tests, meaningful importer/network tests, existing isolated database authorization tests when available, browser desktop/mobile regressions (login interruption, Back, repeated taps, import/review and inbox privacy) and Android/iOS/web exports run. Record failures and physical-device/native-build limits truthfully.
